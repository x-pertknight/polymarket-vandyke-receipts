Reading the fill log by address

Polymarket's exchange writes one fill row per order. When this wallet is the taker, each counterparty's row also carries its address in the taker field. A desk that builds its own parser and credits those rows to the wallet books the counterparties' trades as the wallet's own. Paradigm documented the volume version of this in December 2025.

Receipts on chain Every row naming the wallet

Buys

Crediting taker-field rows adds $16,700 of counterparty buys, 1.5 times the wallet's real buys.

Sale money received

It adds $347,866 of sale proceeds the wallet never received, 60 times its real sales.

This is known exchange behavior, not a new discovery. The fix is to credit each fill row to its maker field only. Applied that way, the fills reproduce all 15 trade receipts to the micro USDC.e. These multiples come from this wallet's trades and are not a general error rate.

Check another wallet

The repository ships a script that credits each fill row to its maker. It reads the fills slice in the repo, a CSV export of OrderFilled rows, or a Polygon RPC that serves archive logs. On the shipped slice only the named wallet is unredacted, so other wallets need the CSV or RPC input. It covers the original exchange contracts only. Polymarket now trades on newer contracts that the script does not read.

python3 -I code/check_wallet.py 0x31a56e9e690c621ed21de08cb559e9524cdb8ed9 \
    --jsonl code/raw/fills_vandyke_txs.jsonl
